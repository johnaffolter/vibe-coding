Study session planner

List topics and a deadline. Each topic gets a first pass, then spaced reviews about 1, 3 and 7 days later (hard topics get an extra early review and a late refresher), packed so no day goes over your limit. Export to your calendar, and reschedule anything you miss.

Add “| hard” or “| easy” after a topic to change how often it comes back. No tag means medium. Ctrl/⌘+Enter builds the plan.
